## Letter of Intent — VIRLUX Pilot Program

**Between:** Virlux Inc. (“VIRLUX”)  
**And:** __________________________ (“Customer”)  
**Effective date:** __________________

### 1. Purpose

Customer will pilot VIRLUX cross-border B2B payment services to evaluate fit for international supplier and contractor payments.

### 2. Pilot term

**90 days** from Effective date, unless terminated earlier by either party with **14 days’ written notice**.

### 3. Services

- Dashboard access for Customer’s authorized users  
- CAD funding via Interac e-Transfer (manual confirmation by VIRLUX ops during pilot)  
- FX quotes and stablecoin settlement where configured (sandbox or production per mutual agreement)  
- Team roles, maker-checker approvals, audit trail  

### 4. Fees

- **____%** per completed payment (standard published rate: 1%)  
- No setup fee during pilot  

### 5. Volume cap

Maximum **$________ CAD** equivalent outbound per calendar month during pilot. VIRLUX may pause sends above cap.

### 6. Customer obligations

- Complete KYC/KYB accurately and promptly  
- Use exact Interac reference codes provided in dashboard  
- Designate approver(s) for payments above threshold  
- No use for sanctions evasion, fraud, or unlawful activity  

### 7. Data & confidentiality

Each party will protect the other’s confidential information. Privacy practices per VIRLUX Privacy Policy at `/privacy`.

### 8. No guarantee

Pilot is **best-effort**. VIRLUX does not guarantee uninterrupted service, specific settlement times, or regulatory outcomes. **Not financial or legal advice.**

### 9. Termination

Either party may terminate on 14 days’ notice. On termination, pending Interac deposits will be handled per ops SOP; settled balances remain subject to Terms.

### 10. Next steps after pilot

If successful, parties will negotiate a standard commercial agreement. If not, pilot ends without obligation to continue.
