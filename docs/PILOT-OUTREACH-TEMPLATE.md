# SME pilot outreach template

Target ICP: Canadian SME, $500K–$5M revenue, pays international suppliers (PH or US corridor).

## Offer (design partner)

- **Duration:** 90 days
- **Pricing:** 1% flat fee on international payments
- **Volume cap:** $50,000 CAD per rolling 30 days (enforced in product)
- **Corridor:** One corridor — Philippines (default) or US (by agreement)
- **Support:** Founder-led onboarding + feedback calls

## Email draft

**Subject:** Design partner — simpler international payments for [Company]

Hi [Name],

I'm building VIRLUX — international business payments for Canadian companies that pay overseas suppliers.

We're looking for **3 design partners** to run real payment workflows on our live dashboard (not a slide deck). The pilot includes:

- Transparent **1%** fee shown before you confirm
- Fund in **CAD via Interac**
- **Team approvals** for payments over your threshold
- One corridor: **[PH / US]** for the pilot period
- Volume cap: **$50K CAD / month** while we refine the product with you

If your finance team sends to [Philippines / US] suppliers today, I'd love a 20-minute walkthrough: [Calendly link or virlux.com/demo].

Best,  
[Founder name]  
virlux.com · app.virlux.com
