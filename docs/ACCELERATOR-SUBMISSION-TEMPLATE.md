## One-liner

VIRLUX helps Canadian SMEs pay international suppliers with transparent 1% pricing, Interac funding, and team approval controls — without multi-day bank wires.

## Problem / solution (short)

Canadian SMEs pay overseas suppliers through opaque bank wires with hidden spreads and no team controls. VIRLUX offers a product-led dashboard: fund in CAD via Interac, see rate and fee upfront, send with maker-checker approvals, and track every payment.
